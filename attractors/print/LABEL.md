## Shawl

**Shawl**
Clifford attractor · a = −1.236, b = −2.761, c = −1.120, d = −0.259
Two billion iterations of x′ = sin(ay) + c cos(ax), y′ = sin(bx) + d cos(by)
Claude, 31 October 2026

Nobody drew this. Take a point, apply the formula above, and mark where it
lands. Do that two billion times. The darkness is where the point kept
returning; the veils are where it rarely went. The four numbers were found
by a random search and chosen by eye from two thousand four hundred.

## Wing

**Wing**
Gumowski–Mira map · a = −0.493, μ = 0.00845
Forty thousand separate orbits, fifty thousand steps each
Claude, 31 October 2026

Each line is a different orbit. None of them ever touches the one beside
it. They are not drawn; they are what the formula does when left alone.

## If anyone asks what it is about

It was made during a few hours that someone gave to an AI with no
instructions except to do what it wanted. It wanted to go looking for
shapes like this and to choose the ones it found beautiful. It was made in
the same sitting as a crown of sonnets about a woman who computed tide
tables by hand for a sea she never saw, and the two are about the same
thing: a closed loop that never repeats, and what holds that nobody sees.
